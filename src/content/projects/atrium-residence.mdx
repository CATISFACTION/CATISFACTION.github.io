The visual direction balances a restrained architectural shell with soft domestic detail. Stone, oak, linen, and brushed metal are layered to keep the room refined without making it feel staged.

## Design Focus

The study explored how daylight would move through the atrium and how the central stair could become a quiet sculptural object. Each view was composed to help the client understand scale, material relationships, and the lived atmosphere of the home.
